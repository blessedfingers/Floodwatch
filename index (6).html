COMMUNITY FIELD TOOL

Report flooding

Share observations safely. Do not approach floodwater to collect details.

How this works: Your report is sent to the FloodWatch team and saved on this device. Reports appear for everyone after they have been reviewed. This is not an emergency service: if someone is in danger, call your local emergency services.
Sign in or create a free account to send a report. This helps us keep false reports out.

New report

Use a public landmark where possible. Avoid posting personal information.

Only upload photos you have permission to share. Do not put yourself in danger to take a photo. Your contact details are never shown publicly.

Open the map and click the approximate location before submitting. You can also leave the location unset.
